306A Answer Keys and Explanations

Numbered to match the practice tests in your printed study guide.

Practice Test 1 Answer Key

  1. The correct answer is D (The product is flammable and can catch fire easily.): The flame pictogram on a WHMIS 2015 supplier label identifies flammable products, which can catch fire easily. The corrosion pictogram, not the flame, warns of skin and metal damage. An oxidizer is a different hazard class shown by a flame over a circle, and aquatic toxicity uses the fish and tree pictogram.
  2. The correct answer is A (Shut down the equipment and apply a personal lock and tag to each energy-isolating device.): Lock-out and tag-out requires isolating every energy source before work begins, and a pump driven by both electricity and steam needs a lock on each isolating device. A warning sign is not a lock, and disconnecting piping before isolation exposes the worker to the energy. The correct sequence always verifies the energy is dead before the work starts.
  3. The correct answer is C (Shoring, shielding, or sloping that meets the excavation requirements.): A trench deeper than about 1.2 m in loose soil must be protected with shoring, a trench box, or a sloped wall before entry. A ladder and a spotter are good practice but they do not stop a cave-in. A trench box set on the surface protects nothing, and barricades only keep people from falling in.
  4. The correct answer is B (Type K copper tube with the heaviest wall of the common types.): Type K has the thickest wall of the standard copper types and is the right choice for underground water services that must resist crushing. Type L is the common interior wall, and Type M is the thinnest and is not allowed underground. DWV copper is not rated for pressure service.
  5. The correct answer is B (A CPVC cement rated for the higher service temperature.): CPVC pipe carries hot water, so the solvent cement must be the CPVC formulation rated for that temperature. PVC cement is for PVC, ABS cement is for ABS, and a general-purpose cement is not rated for the hot water service. Using the wrong cement produces a joint that can fail under temperature.
  6. The correct answer is C (50 mm measured from the crown weir to the top of the dip.): The National Plumbing Code requires a minimum trap seal depth of 50 mm, measured from the crown weir to the top of the dip. A 25 mm seal is too shallow, and 12 mm would let sewer gas pass through. A 75 mm seal is deeper than the minimum, so it is not the required answer.
  7. The correct answer is A (3 in, the code minimum for a water closet drain.): A water closet drain is not smaller than 3 in in the plumbing code. The closet flange matches the 3 in drain, and 4 in is the minimum building sewer size, not the fixture drain size. A 2 in drain is too small for a water closet, and 1 1/2 in is the size for a bathtub.
  8. The correct answer is D (It admits air to the drainage system so pressure stays balanced.): The vent admits air to the drainage system so the pressure stays balanced and the trap seals are protected. Without the vent, the flush creates a vacuum that siphons the bathtub trap. The vent does not carry waste, supply water, or provide cleaning access.
  9. The correct answer is A (A stainless steel coupling with a rubber sleeve and clamps.): No-hub cast iron is joined with a stainless steel coupling that clamps a rubber sleeve over the two plain pipe ends. The lead and oakum joint belongs to hub and spigot pipe, not no-hub. Threaded unions and solvent-cemented adapters do not fit cast iron soil pipe.
  10. The correct answer is C (A cleanout installed at the base of the stack.): A cleanout at the base of the stack gives a drain cleaner access to the point where stoppages collect. An access panel at the second floor does not reach the base, a test tee at the top serves testing not cleaning, and a floor drain does not provide stack access.
  11. The correct answer is B (2 in, not less than one half the stack size.): A stack vent must be at least one half the size of the stack it protects in common code practice, so a 4 in stack takes a 2 in stack vent. The vent cannot be reduced below that minimum, and it is not required to match the full stack size. The 1 1/4 in minimum vent size applies to fixture vents, not stack vents.
  12. The correct answer is A (A pressure reducing valve set to the building design pressure.): The code sets a maximum interior pressure, commonly about 550 kPa or 80 psi, and a PRV brings the 700 kPa supply down to the design pressure. A throttled gate valve is not a pressure regulator, and a check valve does not reduce pressure. A relief valve protects against overpressure but does not regulate it.
  13. The correct answer is D (It absorbs the water expansion in the closed system.): A backflow preventer at the service makes the system closed, so heated water has nowhere to expand and the pressure climbs. The expansion tank absorbs that expansion and protects the water heater and piping. It does not raise pressure, stop the preventer from leaking, or remove air.
  14. The correct answer is D (An air gap between the outlet and the rim.): An air gap is the physical space between the water outlet and the flood level rim of the receptor, and it is the most reliable backflow protection. The mechanical devices, the RP assembly, the vacuum breaker, and the double check assembly, all rely on moving parts. A submerged outlet with no gap is a direct cross-connection.
  15. The correct answer is B (The pump start pressure and the pump stop pressure.): The pressure switch starts the pump at the cut-in pressure and stops it at the cut-out pressure, commonly 30/50 or 40/60 psi. The tank air charge and water volume are separate settings, and the switch does not control well depth or flow rate. A check valve controls direction, not the pump cycle.
  16. The correct answer is C (Run to a safe location and remain open to the atmosphere.): The T&P valve discharge pipe must terminate at a safe location, such as a floor drain, with the end open and visible. Connecting it to the drainage system, capping it, or returning it to the inlet would block the discharge and defeat the safety device. The valve must never be capped or valved off.
  17. The correct answer is C (12 in measured to the centre of the closet flange.): The standard water closet rough-in is 12 in from the finished wall to the centre of the closet flange outlet. The 10 in and 14 in values are non-standard offsets for special fixtures, and 16 in to the flange edge is not the code dimension. The rough-in is measured to the centre of the outlet.
  18. The correct answer is A (An air gap in the drain line.): A dishwasher drain must be protected against back siphonage with an air gap in the drain line. A check valve is not an approved protection for dishwasher drains, and a P-trap on the supply line does nothing. The protection is on the drain side, where the cross-connection exists.
  19. The correct answer is D (The hardness measured in grains per gallon.): Water softener sizing starts with the hardness of the water, measured in grains per gallon, multiplied by the daily use. The pH, chlorine level, and turbidity do not determine softener capacity. Hardness is the calcium and magnesium content that the softener resin removes.
  20. The correct answer is B (The pressure climbs because the expanding water has nowhere to go.): A waterlogged expansion tank has lost its air cushion, so the heated water cannot compress the air and the system pressure climbs. The relief valve may open, but it is protecting the system, not regulating the pressure. The pump, the cycle, and the temperature setting do not cause the climb.
  21. The correct answer is C (A circulator sized for the system flow and head.): The pump that moves water in a hydronic system is called a circulator, and it is sized for the flow and the head of the system. A jet pump lifts from a well, and a submersible pump sits in water. The circulator is a wet rotor pump in most hydronic installations.
  22. The correct answer is D (An aquastat that senses the boiler water temperature.): The aquastat senses the boiler water temperature and starts and stops the burner to hold the setpoint. A low water cut-off senses water level, a pressure switch senses pressure, and a room thermostat senses air temperature. The boiler temperature control is the aquastat.
  23. The correct answer is B (They increase the surface area that gives up heat to the air.): The fins on a baseboard element increase the surface area that transfers heat from the copper tube to the air. They do not carry pressure, support the tube, or protect it from corrosion. More surface area means more heat given up, which is the whole point of the fin design.
  24. The correct answer is C (A diameter index safety system with unique thread sizes.): The Diameter Index Safety System gives each medical gas its own thread size and shape so a hose from one gas cannot be connected to another. Labels help, but the physical protection is the connection design. A padlock and a pressure gauge do not prevent a wrong connection.
  25. The correct answer is A (It catches dirt and moisture so they do not reach the appliance.): A sediment trap, or drip leg, is a vertical pocket at the appliance inlet where dirt and moisture drop out of the gas flow. It does not raise the connection, hold the test gauge, or support the connector. Protecting the appliance from debris is its only job.
  26. The correct answer is A (Match the fixture unit total to the size table, then check the developed length and pressure drop.): Water piping is sized by matching the supply fixture unit total to the code tables, then confirming the size works for the developed length and available pressure. Counting fixtures and adding pipe sizes is not a code method. The service size is never based on the sewer size.
  27. The correct answer is B (Ream the inside of the tube and clean the outside with emery cloth.): After cutting copper tube, the inside is reamed to remove the burr and the outside is cleaned so the solder joint can bond. Flux goes on after cleaning, and threading applies to steel, not copper. Oil would contaminate the joint and cause it to fail.
  28. The correct answer is D (5 in, found by multiplying the run by the grade.): The drop equals the run times the grade: 40 ft times 1/8 in per ft equals 5 in. The other values come from using the wrong factor or the wrong operation. Grade and run are multiplied, never divided or added.
  29. The correct answer is C (Attempt to start the pump to verify the energy is isolated.): After the pump is locked out and tagged out, the worker tries to start it to prove the energy is truly isolated. Removing the maintenance tag before the work is done breaks the protection, and a second tag does not verify anything. Bleeding pressure is part of the work, not the verification.
  30. The correct answer is A (5 in, calculated as the run times the slope.): The drop over the run is the slope times the length: 1/8 in per ft over 40 ft gives 5 in. The drawing shows the slope and the run, and the calculation uses those two numbers. The pipe size, depth, and bedding do not enter the drop calculation.
  31. The correct answer is D (Fill slowly with the air vents open, then purge each loop and top up the pressure.): A hydronic system is filled slowly with the vents open so air is pushed out, then each loop is purged and the pressure is topped up. Filling fast traps air, and closing the vents before the air is out leaves pockets. The system is never filled to the relief valve setting.
  32. The correct answer is C (Place the wax ring on the flange, set the closet, and tighten the bolts evenly.): The water closet is set over the wax ring on the flange and the bolts are tightened evenly to pull the bowl down without rocking. Caulking the base first seals nothing, and tightening one bolt fully can crack the bowl. The wax ring seals the horn to the flange.
  33. The correct answer is D (Braze the joints with the filler metal specified for medical gas piping.): Medical gas piping is cleaned copper joined by brazing with the filler metal specified for the service. Solvent cement, soldering flux, and threaded copper are not permitted in medical gas systems. The joints must also be leak tested and the system tested for purity.
  34. The correct answer is C (The load on each leg increases as the angle from vertical increases.): When the legs of a sling spread wider, the angle from vertical increases and the tension in each leg rises above half the load. A sling rated for a vertical lift cannot carry the same load at a wide angle. The tag line does not carry the load.
  35. The correct answer is B (Plug the lower end, fill the system to the highest point, and watch the level.): A water test plugs the lower end of the DWV system, fills it to the highest point, and watches for a drop in level that would indicate a leak. Pressurizing with air is the air test, and running water through fixtures does not prove watertightness. Filling individual traps is not a system test.
  36. The correct answer is C (The valve is installed in the correct flow direction and set to the building pressure.): A PRV must be installed with its flow arrow pointing in the direction of flow and adjusted to the required building pressure. A strainer is typically installed upstream, not downstream, and a PRV does not bypass the main shut-off. Reversing the valve destroys its regulation.
  37. The correct answer is D (Install an air gap on the countertop.): The dishwasher drain needs an air gap so sewage cannot be siphoned back into the machine. Connecting directly to the tailpiece, tying into the vent, or running the drain downhill without a loop leaves a cross-connection. The air gap is the positive break.
  38. The correct answer is B (Set each one to the calculated position and measure the flow through each zone.): Balancing valves are set to the calculated position and the flow is measured so every zone receives its design flow. Opening them all lets the shortest zones steal the water, and closing the short zones completely is not balancing. Removing the valves eliminates the adjustment.
  39. The correct answer is C (Between the trap weir and the stack, upstream of the trap arm limit.): The individual vent must connect to the drain between the trap weir and the stack, within the maximum trap arm length, so it protects that trap. Connecting downstream of the stack vent protects nothing, and a connection at the stack base is below the fixture. The vent never connects to the trap itself.
  40. The correct answer is D (Post a lookout, keep the ventilation running, and maintain a rescue plan.): Confined space entry requires the atmosphere to be continuously ventilated, a lookout at the opening, and a rescue plan ready before anyone enters. Closing the opening and turning off ventilation make the space more dangerous. Approval is obtained before entry, not after the worker is inside.
  41. The correct answer is A (Prime both surfaces, apply cement to the pipe and socket, and push the pipe fully home with a twist.): Solvent-cemented joints are primed, coated with cement on the pipe end and the socket, and pushed fully home with a slight twist so the pipe bottoms out. Cement on one surface only, or a partial insertion, leaves a weak joint. Beading cement on the outside seals nothing.
  42. The correct answer is A (The tank is sized for the system volume and pre-charged to the cold fill pressure.): The expansion tank must be sized for the water volume and temperature range of the system and pre-charged to the cold fill pressure. It is installed on the cold water side, not the hot outlet, and it is never valved off from the system. A tank filled solid with water has no expansion capacity.
  43. The correct answer is B (Terminate it at a floor drain with the end visible and not threaded.): The T&P discharge pipe terminates at a safe location like a floor drain, with the end open and visible so a discharge can be seen. Connecting it to the cold inlet, running it into the vent, or capping it would block the safety discharge. The end is not threaded so nothing can be attached to it.
  44. The correct answer is A (At the appliance inlet, rising vertically from the drop leg.): The sediment trap is installed at the appliance inlet as a vertical drop that catches debris, and the appliance connection rises from it. At the meter it would catch nothing, at the high point there is no debris, and at every direction change it would be impractical. The drip leg protects the appliance.
  45. The correct answer is B (The relief valve between the two check valves.): In a reduced pressure principle assembly, the relief valve sits between the two check valves and opens to the atmosphere if the downstream check leaks, discharging water instead of letting contamination pass. The shut-off valves isolate the assembly, and the test cocks are for testing. The first check valve is upstream protection.
  46. The correct answer is A (The zone valve is closed internally and is not passing water.): If the pipe is hot on both sides of a powered zone valve but the zone stays cold, the valve is not passing water internally. The thermostat and valve motor are working, so the fault is in the valve itself. The aquastat and expansion tank would affect the whole system, not one zone.
  47. The correct answer is C (Return the product and obtain a labelled container or the SDS.): A hazardous product without a supplier label cannot be identified safely, and the plumber must return it and get a labelled container or the SDS. Using it based on the container markings alone is guesswork, and transferring it into another container creates a new hazard. Odour is never a way to identify a chemical.
  48. The correct answer is A (Crimp or expansion fittings using the correct matched tooling.): PEX is joined with crimp or expansion fittings made with the matching tooling, and these methods need no flame, which suits a finished basement. Solvent cement and soldering do not apply to PEX, and threading PEX is impossible. The tooling must match the fitting brand and size.
  49. The correct answer is A (The breaker is installed above the highest downstream outlet.): An atmospheric vacuum breaker must be installed above the highest downstream outlet so it opens to the atmosphere when the pressure drops. Below the outlet it stays flooded, and under continuous pressure it fails. It is never buried, because it must open to the air.
  50. The correct answer is C (A cleanout accessible at the change of direction.): A change of direction greater than about 45 degrees in a horizontal DWV branch requires a cleanout so a stoppage can be cleared. An automatic vent, a trap primer, and a test tee do not provide access for cleaning. The cleanout is installed and left accessible.
  51. The correct answer is D (On the cold water supply line to the water heater.): The expansion tank is connected to the cold water supply line to the water heater, where it can absorb the expansion of the heated water in a closed system. On the hot outlet it would see the hottest water, on the T&P line it would block the safety valve, and on the drain it serves no purpose.
  52. The correct answer is D (The concentration is high enough to protect the system at the coldest design temperature.): The antifreeze concentration must protect the system at the coldest expected temperature, and it is verified with a refractometer each season. Topping up the expansion tank, draining the glycol for winter, and mixing glycol into the domestic water are all wrong. The system stays charged all winter.
  53. The correct answer is D (The condensate traps on the drops and mains are not being drained.): Water at the tools with a working refrigerated dryer usually means the condensate traps are full and not being drained. The receiver size, the regulator setting, and the filter position do not put water in the line. Condensate must be removed at the low points.
  54. The correct answer is B (Complete the hot work permit, clear the combustibles, and place a fire extinguisher nearby.): Before brazing, the plumber completes the hot work permit, clears the combustibles from the area, and has a fire extinguisher within reach. Closing the door and covering the materials with a tarp still leaves the fire hazard. The permit is completed before the work starts.
  55. The correct answer is C (The coupling is torqued to spec and the pipe is supported near the joint.): Hubless couplings are tightened to the manufacturer's torque and the pipe is supported near the joint so the coupling does not carry the pipe weight. Soldering, threading, and solvent cementing do not apply to cast iron. The coupling clamps a rubber sleeve over the plain ends.
  56. The correct answer is B (Two to three threads remain exposed and the joint is wrench tight.): A correct threaded joint shows two to three threads beyond the fitting and is tightened with a wrench, not by hand. No visible threads means the pipe is in too far and can split the fitting, and cross threading is never acceptable. Sealant goes on the male threads before assembly.
  57. The correct answer is A (Manifold, where a central manifold feeds a dedicated line to each fixture.): A manifold layout runs a dedicated line from the central manifold to each fixture, giving each fixture its own shut-off at the manifold. Trunk and branch feeds groups from one main, a loop returns to the source, and a riser feeds floors. The dedicated-line system is the manifold.
  58. The correct answer is B (A supply line sized for the flush valve and a shut-off at the valve.): A flush valve water closet needs a supply line sized for the flush valve demand and a shut-off valve at the valve. A 3/8 in supply is far too small for a flush valve, and a gravity tank uses a different supply arrangement. A flush valve uses cold water only.
  59. The correct answer is A (The smoke test, which shows smoke escaping from open or broken connections.): The smoke test pumps a visible smoke into the system so escaping smoke reveals open or broken vent connections. The air test holds pressure, the water test fills the system, and the deflection test checks flexible pipe shape. Only the smoke test shows the vent paths visually.
  60. The correct answer is A (Isolation flanges and valves on both sides of the pump.): Isolation flanges with valves on both sides let a circulator be removed while the system stays full. A union on the suction side only still drains the discharge side, and threaded adapters or a flanged spool do not isolate both ports. The pump is isolated, then removed.
  61. The correct answer is A (One and a half times the working pressure or the code minimum.): A water system is pressure tested at one and a half times the working pressure, or the code minimum where that is higher, and the pressure must hold. Street pressure plus a margin is not a test standard, and testing at the relief valve setting is not a leak test. The code sets the test pressure.
  62. The correct answer is B (Chemical resistant gloves and eye protection rated for solvents.): Cleaning a solvent spill requires gloves and eye protection rated for chemicals, because solvent cement penetrates leather and cotton. Leather gloves are for heat, and a dust mask does not stop solvent vapours. The protection matches the hazard, which is chemical contact.
  63. The correct answer is B (Lift the test lever briefly so water discharges, then let the valve reseat.): The T&P valve is tested by lifting the test lever so water discharges briefly, then releasing it to reseat. Heating the tank until the valve opens and pressurizing past the setting stress the system, and removing the valve is not a field test. The lever test confirms the valve is not seized.
  64. The correct answer is D (Open the manual vent on the unit while the system pressure pushes the air out.): Air is removed from a baseboard unit by opening the manual vent and letting the system pressure push the air out until water appears. Draining the whole loop is wasteful, and closing valves or raising the temperature does not remove the trapped air. The vent is at the high point of the unit.
  65. The correct answer is A (At the property line on the street side of the meter.): The curb stop is installed at the property line, on the street side of the meter, so the authority can shut the service off from outside. Inside the building it would be a building shut-off, not a curb stop, and at the main it would be the corporation stop. The curb stop is between the main and the meter.
  66. The correct answer is D (The tube is cut square, deburred, and inserted to the depth mark.): A press-fit connection needs a square, deburred tube end inserted fully to the depth mark so the O-ring seals. Flaring is for flare fittings, threading is for steel, and heating is for bending. A rough end damages the O-ring and the joint leaks.
  67. The correct answer is B (2 in, the minimum wet vent size for a water closet.): A wet vent serving a water closet is commonly a minimum of 2 in, and the water closet connection must meet the code distance rules. The 1 1/2 in tub arm is not a vent, the 3 in water closet drain is the fixture drain, and 4 in is the sewer size. The wet vent is larger than a fixture vent because it drains too.
  68. The correct answer is B (A pressure balancing or thermostatic mixing valve.): A pressure balancing or thermostatic mixing valve keeps the shower temperature steady when the hot and cold pressures change, preventing scalds. A two-handle globe valve does not balance pressure, and a check valve on one supply does nothing. The mixing valve is the scald protection.
  69. The correct answer is B (The base is 1 unit out from the wall for every 4 units of height.): A straight ladder is set at about a 4 to 1 angle, so the base is about 1 unit out for every 4 units of height, and it extends above the landing. Flush against the wall, vertical, or resting on the ceiling finish are all unstable setups. The angle gives the ladder its stability.
  70. The correct answer is C (A low water cut-off that interrupts the burner circuit.): The low water cut-off stops the burner if the boiler water level drops, protecting the boiler from firing without water. The aquastat controls temperature, the relief valve controls pressure, and the flow switch proves circulation. A boiler that fires with low water can be destroyed.
  71. The correct answer is B (A reduced pressure principle backflow assembly.): An irrigation system that injects fertilizer creates a high hazard, and the code requires the highest level of mechanical protection, the reduced pressure principle assembly. An air gap is not practical at an irrigation connection, an atmospheric vacuum breaker is not rated for this hazard, and a single check valve is never enough.
  72. The correct answer is D (4 in, the minimum size where water closets discharge.): A building sewer that receives water closet discharge is a minimum of 4 in in common code practice. Three inch is the fixture drain size, 6 in is for larger loads, and 2 in is far too small. The sewer is sized for the fixture unit load and the minimum size.
  73. The correct answer is A (21 in, the standard lavatory supply valve height.): Countertop lavatory supply valves are commonly roughed in at 21 in to the centre of the valves above the finished floor. The countertop is higher, the drain outlet is nearby but not the supply height, and 36 in would suit a wall-hung fixture. The rough-in is set from the finished floor.
  74. The correct answer is B (At the low points of the mains and at the base of each drop.): Condensate in a compressed air system collects at the low points of the mains and at the base of each drop, so the collection points and traps are installed there. The highest points hold air, and the compressor intake and regulators are not condensate collection points. Moisture is drained where it settles.
  75. The correct answer is A (It keeps hot water available at the fixtures without waiting for fresh draw.): A recirculation return line and pump keep hot water moving so it is available at the fixtures quickly, instead of going cold in the pipes. It does not raise cold water pressure, drain the lines, or filter the water. The return loop saves water and wait time.
  76. The correct answer is C (Purge the air from the loop through the manifold air vent.): A radiant loop that will not heat with the valve open is usually air bound, and purging the air through the manifold vent restores the flow. Replacing the loop, raising the boiler temperature, and closing the other loops treat symptoms. Air is purged first.
  77. The correct answer is D (Watch the area for a period of time to catch a delayed fire.): After hot work, the plumber watches the area for a period, commonly about 30 minutes, because a fire can smoulder and flare later. Closing the permit early, removing the extinguisher, and wetting the joints do not cover the delayed-fire risk. The fire watch is part of the permit.
  78. The correct answer is D (A trap seal primer that adds water automatically.): A trap seal primer adds water to a rarely used trap automatically so the seal does not evaporate. A deeper trap still evaporates, an automatic vent does not add water, and a check valve is not a trap device. The primer is connected to the water supply.
  79. The correct answer is A (They shut off the gas to one area so it can be serviced without draining the whole system.): Zone valves isolate one area of a medical gas system so it can be serviced while the rest of the system stays in service. They do not regulate pressure, convert gas, or vent the system. The zone valve is the isolation point for a nursing unit.
  80. The correct answer is D (A water hammer arrestor on the machine supply lines.): A water hammer arrestor absorbs the pressure surge when the washing machine valve closes quickly. Raising the pressure makes the hammer worse, larger lines do not stop the shock, and a check valve does not absorb surges. The arrestor is installed on the machine supply.
  81. The correct answer is C (A dielectric union separating the copper from the steel.): Copper and galvanized steel in contact with water form a galvanic cell, and a dielectric union insulates the two metals to stop the corrosion. A brass nipple is a common but wrong shortcut, and rubber couplings and compression fittings are not dielectric connections. The union isolates the metals.
  82. The correct answer is C (2 in, not less than one half the size of the stack.): The stack vent must be at least one half the size of the stack it protects, so a 4 in stack takes a 2 in stack vent minimum. It cannot be reduced below that minimum, and it does not need to match the stack size. The 1 1/4 in figure is the fixture vent minimum.
  83. The correct answer is C (A refractometer that reads the glycol concentration.): A refractometer measures the glycol concentration in the system water, which confirms the freeze protection. A pressure gauge reads pressure, a thermometer reads temperature, and a hydrometer is not the tool for glycol. The concentration must protect the system at the coldest design temperature.
  84. The correct answer is A (The pre-charge is set about 2 psi below the pump cut-in pressure.): A well pressure tank is pre-charged to about 2 psi below the pump cut-in pressure, with the tank drained, so the bladder has the right air cushion. A tank full of water is waterlogged, charging to the cut-out pressure is wrong, and venting the tank destroys the cushion. The pre-charge is set with the pump off and the tank drained.
  85. The correct answer is A (Pre-filter, membrane, storage tank, post-filter, faucet.): A reverse osmosis system flows feed water through the pre-filter, then the membrane, into the storage tank, through the post-filter, and out the faucet. The membrane must be protected by the pre-filter, and the post-filter polishes the stored water. Any other order plugs the membrane or delivers untreated water.
  86. The correct answer is D (Above the flood level rim of the sink it serves.): The vent connection must be above the flood level rim so a backed-up system cannot fill the vent with waste. At the trap weir, below the floor, or at the backsplash height, the vent could flood. The flood rim is the reference for the vent height.
  87. The correct answer is A (Remove the sling from service and use a sling that has no damage.): A cut in the webbing destroys the sling's rating, and the damaged sling must be removed from service and replaced. Tape, half-rating, and flipping the sling do not restore its strength. Damaged rigging is never used.
  88. The correct answer is D (The supports maintain the required slope and spacing for the pipe.): The hangers must maintain the required slope and spacing so the DWV pipe drains and does not sag. Supporting only at fittings lets the pipe sag between them, and gripping the pipe tightly stops expansion. The slope is set and held by the supports.
  89. The correct answer is B (Lift the test lever while the system is at pressure and watch for discharge.): The pressure relief valve is tested by lifting the lever with the system at pressure and confirming water discharges and the valve reseats. Removing the valve, closing its discharge, and overheating the boiler are not safe tests. The lever test proves the valve is not stuck.
  90. The correct answer is C (Flush the lines and disinfect the system with a chlorine solution.): After the pressure test, a new water system is flushed to remove debris and disinfected with a chlorine solution before service. Draining and capping the outlets leaves the system dead, double pressurizing proves nothing, and running fixtures cold does not disinfect. The flush and disinfection are the final preparation.
  91. The correct answer is D (Brace the plugs securely so the air pressure cannot blow them out.): Air test plugs must be braced or secured because the air pressure pushes against them and can blow them out of the pipe.
